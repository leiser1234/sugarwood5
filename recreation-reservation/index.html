Required acknowledgments
  1. Parties may be scheduled for anytime during Rec. area open hours of 8:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. (outside pavilion) or advertised pool operating hours but must end (including clean-up) 30 minutes prior to these closing times.
  2. Only adult SHA Members in good standing may host parties at the Rec. area and must be in attendance during the entirety of the event. Non-SHA member guest(s) may attend only during the hours of the event. All SHA rules, guidelines and policies apply.
  3. The SHA member accepts responsibility for cleaning the pavilion area both prior to and after the event, and to pay any applicable lifeguard fees (described below). SHA Member also accepts full responsibility to pay for any and all damages to SHA property during the scheduled event, and will be billed for these damages following the event if deemed necessary by the Board of Directors. SHA Member accepts these terms by signing this document below.
  4. Separate SHA Lifeguard(s) @ $15/hour must be hired for all parties with pool use and meet the following criteria:
    • One guard for up to 25 persons (not counting children under two years of age);
    • Two lifeguards for 26 to 35 persons (not counting children under two years of age);
    • Maximum number of guests not to exceed 35 persons over the age of two.
    • Lifeguard(s) for the reservation will be provided by the Pool Manager. Reservation holder will pay the lifeguard(s) directly upon completion of the party.
  5. No Glass or Ceramic items of any kind are permitted in the pool area.
  6. No Alcoholic Beverages are permitted in the pool and facility area.
