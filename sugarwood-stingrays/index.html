Sugarwood Stingrays

A summer swim program focused on fun, growth, confidence, friendship, and sportsmanship.

Summer swimming

Build skills. Build confidence. Have fun.

The Sugarwood Stingrays Swim Team (SWSR) is an independent organization from the Sugarwood Homeowners Association. The public site identifies the Stingrays as a Division 2 team competing under GKAISA.

SWSR provides summer swim opportunities for children at all levels, with an emphasis on confidence, endurance, self-discipline, friendship, and good sportsmanship.

Visit the Stingrays team site Visit GKAISA

Separate organization: Swim-team schedules, registration, eligibility, and fees are managed by SWSR. Use the team website for current details.
