A Farragut treasure

Connected to everything. Tucked away from it all.

Sugarwood is located off Kingston Pike in Farragut, Tennessee. The neighborhood is close to West Knoxville amenities while maintaining the quiet setting that has made it a desirable place to call home.

The current public site describes Sugarwood as two miles from McFee Park, four miles from Turkey Creek and Interstate 40, 18 miles from Oak Ridge and McGhee Tyson Airport, and 19 miles from downtown Knoxville.
